## The new region: Sumi Marsh

Beat Hōshō, and the gold folding screen at the end of his hall folds back
onto a stone stair going down. Walk east through it and you are in **Sumi
Marsh**, home of **Shimeri, the Sodden General**. It is about 404 m long,
near the size of the market and the bamboo.

**Quickest ways in** (they skip ahead):
- `--marsh`: the start of the marsh (no Wax Coat yet)
- `--mere`: the Wax-Maker's island, waxed
- `--shimeri`: the shrine before Shimeri's gate (add `--met` to skip his
  intro)

**The look.** A drowned dusk instead of night: a low ceiling of torn cloud,
a band of pale green fog, no moon. Everything that was water is black ink.
There are bone-white dead trees hung with soaked paper streamers, reeds that
part as you push through them, thatched stilt houses and drowned torii. The
only warm light comes from candles, windows and one hearth. Will-o'-wisps
drift between the trees.

**Left to right:**
1. **The Weeping Steps.** Down into the fog, past a half-sunk torii and a
   drowned jizō with candles. Shrine 10.
2. **The Reed Shallows.** Boardwalks over the ink. Wading in shallow ink is
   slow and **soaks ink into your paper** (your ink marks grow; it counts
   toward the endings). Sodden planks sink under you and lotus pads dip.
   Ink frogs live here. Climb the dead tree's branches to a heron's nest
   for cranes.
3. **The Drowned Village.** Stilt houses whose thatched porch roofs are
   the high route (climb the crates). One house has slumped half into the
   ink. Shrine 11 is on a porch, cranes are on a roof, and soaked husks rise
   out of the ink. Two crane stashes here need the Wax Coat: one floating
   under the first porch, and one in a niche under a pour of ink that
   pushes you down until you are waxed.
4. **The Wax-Maker's Island.** An old paper doll in a straw rain cape
   stirs her pot of wax under rust-red haze trees. Come to her and she
   pours wax over you: **the Wax Coat**.
5. **The Black Mere.** Wide deep ink. You swim it. Ink eels hunt in it, and
   the broken aqueduct is a high route with cranes. Shrine 12 is in a reed
   hut on an island.
6. **The Causeway of Lost Names.** Rows of grave posts, drowned stone
   lanterns, limp banners of his army, soaked husk soldiers, and ink pouring
   through a broken arch. Shrine 13.
7. **Shimeri's Post.** A flooded gate fort, knee-deep in ink. Shimeri.

## The Wax Coat (new ability)

Waxed paper floats.
- In **deep water or ink anywhere in the game**, you no longer go under
  and back to the shrine. You float waist-deep, swim slowly, and can jump
  out.
- Ink no longer stains or slows you.
- The Rain's weight runs off you. Ink pours and the town's sluices no
  longer press you down.
- **Reasons to go back:** cranes on floating wreckage that can only be
  swum to, in the town's deep canal water, in the pool under the bamboo
  falls, and on the market's second canal.

## Shimeri, 湿将, the Sodden General (boss)

You allowed Meshy, so he is a Meshy model: a huge armoured samurai of
soaked paper, its painted mask half washed off, dripping ink all the time.
He is posed by hand and carries a long nodachi folded in code. He is slow
and heavy.
- **Cleave:** he raises the blade high over his shoulder, then brings it
  down. Parry it. A wave of ink then runs out along the floor both ways:
  jump it.
- **Low sweep:** he drags the blade back through the ink (watch for the
  wake), then sweeps at your ankles. Jump.
- **Charge:** he lowers his shoulder and wades in fast. Parry it for double
  posture.
- **The weeping sky:** he lifts his face and arms to the Rain, and ink
  falls where stains spread first.
- **He forgets:** part-way through a wind-up he stops, his arms sag and his
  head tilts, as if trying to remember. That is your opening. Then he starts
  a move again, always with its own wind-up from the beginning, so it stays
  fair. He forgets more often after half health.
- From half health he calls up two drowned soldiers out of the ink.
- **Intro (12 s):** along the flooded court to him, slumped. His head
  lifts and he says "Who goes. ... This gate is kept." He raises the blade
  and brings it down into the ink, and his name is painted (湿将) with its
  seal. A curtain of ink pours across the way back.
- **Ending (14 s):** slow motion on the last cut. He kneels, and the ink
  drains out of him, leaving pale paper with his folds showing. He lifts
  his head: "Shimeri. That was my name." His seal flies to you.
- He has his own fight music: slow taiko, a sorrowful shakuhachi, a low
  choir.

## New creatures

- **Ink frog** (a Meshy model; its legs are given bones in code): an
  origami frog soaked black. It croaks. Its tell: it rocks back on its
  hind legs with its throat swelling, then it leaps at you. Parried, it
  flips onto its back.
- **Ink eel** (folded in code from a Meshy picture, so its body can
  writhe): a pale fin cuts through the ink toward you and the ink bulges,
  then it bursts out over you. From the edge of its pool it lunges at you
  on the bank and drops back in. Parried, it falls back belly-up.
- **Sodden husks:** the town's husk soaked dark, rising out of the ink as
  you come near.

## And the next region, begun: Koinobori Skies

Beat Shimeri and his gate swings open; climb out of the fog into the next
region (`--skies` starts there; plan `docs/koinobori_skies_plan.md`).
- **The look:** high, bright and windy, the opposite of the marsh. Cliff
  tops stand out of a sea of torn-paper cloud. Snowy peaks are far off.
  Tall poles fly faded carp streamers that flap in the wind.
- **Giant carp streamers, cut loose, drift between the cliffs:** you ride
  them as moving platforms. Rising air at some cliff edges lifts your
  glide.
- **The Kite-Makers' Eyrie:** a hall of giant kite frames. At its altar a
  great folded shuriken teaches **the Shuriken Dash**: dash in the air and
  you spin like a thrown shuriken, a bit faster and further. The spin tears
  through **paper walls** stretched across the way (a plain dash or a cut
  does nothing to them), and it cuts small paper creatures in your path.
- **Kite husks** ride the wind here, and a new creature, the
  **kamaitachi**: a sickle-weasel of paper riding a whirl of scraps. Its
  tell is a whistle and a coil of scraps where it will come from; then it
  streaks past with its blades out. Parried, it tumbles.
- **The Wind Shrine:** wind-bells along the eave, a bell rope, pinwheels
  along the fence, and ropes from the cliff down into the cloud.
- The Thousand Gates stand on the far skyline.
- **Akane's second duel** at the far end, harder than in the bamboo: more
  health, quicker wind-ups, she turns more of your cuts aside, more
  fox-fires. She has a new move of her own here, the **gale dive**: paper
  swirls at her feet, a gust lifts her, she hangs a moment, then dives at you
  blade first. Parry it. New lines: "Still you do not know what you are."
  and "Higher, then. The Gates will show you."
- Not built yet:
  - the way on to the Thousand Gates.
