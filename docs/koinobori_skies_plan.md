# Koinobori Skies: plan for the region (proposed, for Julian)

Region 5 in `docs/design.md`: "Bright and windy. Giant koi-fish kites drift
across the clouds as moving platforms." Next after Sumi Marsh in story
order (the open middle of the game, `docs/lore.md`).

## How he gets there

Past Shimeri's gate (shut until he is beaten) a long stair of wet stone
climbs out of the marsh's fog. At its top the cloud breaks, and he walks
out into wind and daylight on a cliff above a sea of cloud. Its own place:
`SHIFT` 4000.

## The look (what makes it unlike the others)

- **High, bright and windy**, the opposite of the marsh: a pale washed
  blue sky, a white sun behind thin cloud, a sea of cloud below (torn paper,
  layer on layer), far peaks standing out of it (Yukiyama, snowy, on the
  horizon: where the story goes later).
- **Koinobori:** giant carp streamers, red, black, indigo and gold, fading
  and tattered, flying from tall poles on the cliffs and drifting free in the
  wind. The free ones are the moving platforms: they ride the wind along
  set paths, their bodies fill and slacken, and their tails flick.
- **Paper everywhere in the wind:** streamers, prayer flags, pinwheels on
  the poles, kites cut loose drifting by.
- Not vibrant: the colours of old streamers bleached by sun and rain. The
  Rain falls here too, thinly, in gusts of drizzle out of the cloud.

## The Shuriken Dash

A spinning air-dash: in the air, dash and he spins like a thrown shuriken,
a little further than a dash, cutting through thin paper walls (new
obstacles: torn screens stretched between poles) and through small paper
creatures in his path. It opens ways back in earlier regions (paper walls
added there over shortcuts or crane caches).

## New creatures (proposed)

- **Carp kite (koi)**: a moving platform, not an enemy.
- **Kite husks** (from the grove) return in numbers.
- **Wind wisp (kamaitachi-like)**: a sickle-weasel of folded paper riding
  the gusts, slashing as it passes; its tell is a whistle and a curl of
  paper scraps where it will come from.
