# The Thousand Gates: plan for the region (proposed, for Julian)

Region 6 in `docs/design.md`: "Vertical, relentless. A climb through endless
vermilion torii." Next after Koinobori Skies (Akane: "Higher, then. The
Gates will show you.").

## How he gets there

From the Kite-Fighters' Ring at the end of the Skies, a rope bridge runs
east to the foot of the mountain seen on the skyline, where the first gate
stands. Its own place: `SHIFT` 5000.

## The look

- **A mountain climbed by tunnels of torii**, thousands of them, close
  together: the path runs through them, so the gates frame everything (like
  Fushimi Inari). Their vermilion is old and flaking, with black feet and
  brushed donors' names on their posts.
- Late afternoon turning to dusk as he climbs: warm low sun through the
  gates throws bars of light and shadow across the steps.
- Fox statues (kitsune) with red bibs, stone lanterns, small shrines with
  hundreds of tiny gates piled as offerings.
- The Rain is held off by the gates. Under them it is dry; between them, a
  fine drizzle.

## How it plays (vertical)

The region climbs rather than runs: switchback stairs, walls to cling to
between gate tunnels, ledges, the Crane Glide between spurs, the Shuriken
Dash through paper-walled gate halls, the Fan Gust turning prayer wheels
that lift platforms. A climb from the foot (0 m) to the summit (about 90 m
up), in five terraces, each with its shrine.

## The ability: Flat Fold (proposed)

Fold yourself paper-thin to slip through cracks and under doors
(design.md). In the gates: through the narrow gaps between tightly packed
torii posts into hidden side paths, and under the shut doors of offering
halls. It opens ways back in earlier regions (cracks in walls, shut doors).

## Creatures (proposed)

- **Kitsune statues** that wake: stone foxes that leap down from their
  plinths (tell: their eyes kindle, red).
- **Gatekeepers:** husks in vermilion that wield a torii's crossbeam.
- Akane's foxes: her fox-fires roam the gates on their own.

## The boss (proposed)

**The Gate of Ten Thousand Names**: not a general but the mountain's
summit gate itself, its names crawling off it as ink. Or Akane's third duel
at the summit. To decide with Julian.

## Order of building (when Julian says go)

1. The place: the mountain, the light, the gate tunnels, the way in.
2. The five terraces and their shrines.
3. The Flat Fold and its gaps.
4. The creatures.
5. The summit.
