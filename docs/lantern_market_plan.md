# The Lantern Market: plan for the whole region

Proposed, for Julian to judge. The map is `docs/market_map.png`
(`tools/market_map.py`: edit its lists and run it again).

## Size and shape

- **About 450 m long**: double the bamboo grove (about 225 m). What is built
  today (150 m: the Night Bazaar, the red bridge, the Upper Market, the
  steps) becomes the first third.
- **Three layers**, joined in several places, so there is always more than
  one way on:
  - **the Street** (the main way, stalls and squares);
  - **the Lantern Roofs** above it (stall awnings that bounce, lantern
    strings to pogo, red walkways, rooftops; later a sky route that needs
    the glide and the new Fan Gust);
  - **the Canals** below it (walkways, floating lantern boats, a lock, a
    gold-leaf workshop).
- **Climbs as it goes**: from the river level at the Mist Gate to about
  24 m up at Hōshō's Gate, so the palace on the hilltop draws nearer.

## Routes and shortcuts

- **Three ways through the middle**: the street (fights in the open),
  the roofs (platforming, bouncing, pogo), the canals (quiet, boats, hidden
  cranes). They join at Festival Square, before the Ward, and at the Rise.
- **Shortcuts, opened from the far side**: the canal lock (Canal Quarter
  to shrine 6), the lantern hoist (the Rise down to Festival Square), a rope
  ladder off the Gilded Terraces.
- **After the mini-boss**: the Fan Gust opens new ways back in the earlier
  areas (blow a stall's awning round, turn a waterwheel to raise a boat,
  blow out a fire barrier to a hidden room).

## New enemies (folded paper, like the rest)

- **Chōchin-obake**: a paper lantern torn open into a mouth, one eye, a long
  tongue. Floats, lunges, lashes; pogo-able; cut it and its candle gutters.
- **Gilded guard**: a husk under gold leaf (Hōshō's people feel nothing).
  Blocks cuts from the front until a parry cracks its gold; then it can be
  staggered and finished.
- Old ones return: lantern husks (burning paper), paper husks, paper dogs
  in the canals.

## The mini-boss: a flying dragon that torments the town

As hard as the Hannya (about 30 health, 6 posture, faster at half health),
fought in the Ward of Shut Doors: a square about 40 m wide with rooftops on
two levels around it.

**Chosen by Julian: a ryū (a Japanese dragon).** Proposed: **the
Festival Dragon, 祭龍 (Matsuri-ryū)**. One of the great paper dragons the
market carried through its parades on poles, the Rain soaked it until its
creases forgot what they were; now it lives. Long and serpentine, folded
from red and gold festival paper gone dark and blotched with running ink,
the parade poles still stuck through its body, a bearded lacquered head
with a jaw that hangs crooked, mad bulging eyes, ribbons streaming. Psycho:
it writhes through the Ward of Shut Doors at night, coils round houses and
crushes them, laughs like a cracked bell, and the people bar their doors.
In the tales a ryū rules water and rain; this one brings the Rain down on
the Ward wherever it flies.

Its moves (each with a clear tell first):
- **Coil and crush**: it wraps round a house in the arena (the paper
  crumples, beams snap) and squeezes; the house falls, changing the arena.
  Cutting its coils while it squeezes is the chance to hurt it.
- **Dive through the roofs**: it rears high, its shadow and a roar mark the
  line, then it plunges down through the roofs and across the square. Duck,
  dash under, or parry the head at the last instant (gold) and it rears
  back stunned.
- **Rain breath**: a jet of black rainwater and ink swept across the square
  at chest height (duck) or along the ground (jump); where it lands, puddles
  slow him.
- **Tail lash**: the whole body whips round behind it; parry or block.
- **Lightning in its whiskers**: it calls bolts down on the rooftops in a
  rhythm (stains on the roofs show where first), like the Hannya's ink rain.
- **From half health**: the Rain pours, its flames-of-paper ribbons tear
  loose and fly at him like blades (three in a rhythm, parry them); it
  coils faster and the arena shrinks as houses fall.
- **Stagger**: its head crashes to the ground, jaw open: a finishing cut.
- Being hit by it is **ink** (rain and ink soak his paper; the effect is
  already built).

**Intro**: the street empties, shutters slam one by one down the Ward, a
long shadow slides across the rooftops, a bell cracks, then it pours down
off the roofs, coils round the shrine lantern and laughs; its title painted
with its seal (祭龍).
**After**: it unravels into a heap of wet festival paper; the shutters open
one by one, faces peek out; the fan-maker gives him the **Fan Gust**.

(The first idea, Wanyūdō the burning wheel, was dropped for the dragon.)

## Status (2026-11-24)

Built: all of it except the Rise's hoist shortcut to Festival Square, the
canal lock shortcut and the Fan Gust's awning and waterwheel. The Gilded
Gate leads into Hōshō's hall and fight (`hosho_hall.gd`). Under the
Terraces' bridge is a back alley with a door up to the far terrace (no rope
ladder). The Fan Gust's fire barricade to a hidden room is the burning
store in the Upper Market.
